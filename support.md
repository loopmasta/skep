# Skep Support

Skep is an app for sharing shopping lists, tasks, chat and notes with the people in your household.

## Get help
Email loopmasta@gmail.com, or open an issue at https://github.com/loopmasta/skep/issues. Describe what happened, which iPhone and iOS version you use, and what you expected. Please do not post private information such as the contents of your group.

## Good to know
- Every member needs an iCloud account and must be signed in to iCloud on the device. Skep has no account of its own.
- To join a group, open the invitation link you were sent on a device that is signed in to iCloud.
- Skep requires iOS 26 or later.
- Your data lives in iCloud. To remove it, use Settings, then your name, iCloud, Manage Storage, Skep.

See also the [privacy policy](privacy).
