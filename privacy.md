# Privacy Policy for Skep

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Skep is an app for sharing lists, tasks, messages and notes with the people in your household or team.

## What we collect
Nothing. The developer of Skep does not collect, receive, store or sell any personal data, and Skep contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Where your data lives
Everything you enter in Skep (groups, names, photos, shopping items, tasks, messages, notes and history) is stored in iCloud, using Apple's CloudKit:
- On your own devices.
- In your iCloud account.
- In the iCloud storage of the other members of a group you belong to, because a group is shared with them.

The developer cannot see this data. Apple processes it according to the Apple Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Who can see what
Only the members of a group can see its content. You become a member by accepting an invitation link. Your display name and optional photo are visible to the other members of your groups. Members can leave a group, and the group's creator can remove members or delete the group. Deleting a group deletes it for everyone.

## Notifications
Skep can show local reminders for tasks and notifications when someone mentions you. They are created on your device. Skep also receives silent notifications from iCloud so that it can update in the background.

## Permissions
- **Photos:** only the pictures you choose yourself, through the system photo picker. Skep cannot see the rest of your library.
- **Camera:** only when you tap Take Photo to add a picture to a group, a profile, a note or a shopping item. The picture goes into Skep, not into your photo library, and is stored in iCloud like your other content.
- **Notifications:** asked when first needed.

## Children
Skep is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Your choices
You can delete your content inside the app. Deleting the app does not remove data from iCloud. To remove Skep's data from your iCloud account, use Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Storage ▸ Skep.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
